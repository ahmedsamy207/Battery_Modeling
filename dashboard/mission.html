Assumptions & method

Profile source

  • Demo mission profile — generated in the browser at 1 s resolution from the phase definitions in matlab/Aircraft_Mission.m.
  • X-57: phase powers [34.0, 187.8, 189.3, 156.8, 141.6, 31.4] kW with durations [300, 30, 15, 446, 417, 293] s (NASA X-57 Maxwell profile, Battery_Evaluation_EATS Table 1).
  • eVTOL: phase currents [-14, -56, -280, -200, -112, -28, -14] A × 750 V with durations [300, 180, 60, 180, 720, 180, 180] s.
  • Altitude, ground speed and range are demo assumptions (X-57: 8,000 ft cruise at ≈172 kt; eVTOL: ≈300 m cruise at ≈60 m/s).
  • If results/mission_x57.csv / results/mission_evtol.csv (exported by Aircraft_Mission.m) exist, the page uses that exact MATLAB export instead.

Pack scaling (step5_BuildPackParams.m)

  • 192 cells series × 4 strings parallel → 192S4P.
  • Cell: ≈ 35 Ah, 3.65 V nominal, 2.75–4.20 V window.
  • Pack: ≈ 140 Ah, ≈ 700.8 V, ≈ 98.1 kWh.
  • Capacity per temperature from results/Capacity_vs_T.csv × Np (robust fallback: 34.94 Ah @ 25 °C).
  • 2C current limit ≈ 2 × 140 A ≈ 280 A pack (as in Aircraft_Mission.m).

Browser-side pack estimate

  • Pack current from demand power: I = P × 1000 / V_conv, V_conv = 750 V (MATLAB conversion convention).
  • SOC by Coulomb counting: SOC(t) = SOC₀ − ∫I dt / Q_pack(T), floor at 0.
  • OCV(SOC, T) interpolated from results/OCV_2D_SOCxT.csv (robust fallback: built-in reference curve).
  • Cell resistance R0(T) ≈ representative LUT value (≈1.6–3.2 mΩ across SOC/T; 2.2 mΩ @ 25 °C).
  • Pack voltage = Ns × (OCV − R0 × I/Np). No RC dynamics, no thermal model, no Simscape — a static browser estimate.

Playback & controls

  • requestAnimationFrame playback loop; speed factor applies to dynamic playback only.
  • Timeline slider can be dragged at any time; plots and readouts follow the selected mission time.
  • Playback stops automatically at mission end.
  • Assumption changes (temperature, initial SOC, reserve floor, load factor) rebuild the demo profile and reset to t = 0. They apply to demo profiles only.
  • Reserve margin is carried as the SOC floor (default 20 %); shown as reserve energy in the KPIs.
